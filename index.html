Yes — completely free, no strings. Call or text (725) 710-5841, tell us what you need, and Scott will take a look and give you a clear number up front. No pressure, no obligation.

We're a local handyman service covering Okaloosa & Walton County, Florida — Valparaiso, Niceville, Fort Walton Beach, Shalimar, Mary Esther, Destin, Santa Rosa Beach, and the surrounding areas.

Yes. Floyd's Finishing Touch LLC is a licensed Florida business (Lic. #L25000352562), fully insured, and accredited by the Better Business Bureau of Northwest Florida.

8 AM to 8 PM, seven days a week. We work around your schedule — including flexible scheduling for larger jobs.
